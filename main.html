PyBlast

A survival roguelike game created with pygame-ce.

PyBlast is a survival roguelike game where you defeat horde of enemies and try to survive for 10 minutes. Defeat enemies and level up to obtain upgrades that will help you last long in the cave.

Equipped with dashing to escape enemies and slowing time down to avoid enemies' projectiles, try to fight with the abilities you picked along the way against the endless horde of enemies coming to kill you.
